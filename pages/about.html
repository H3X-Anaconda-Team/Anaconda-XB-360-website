About Anaconda XB 360

Anaconda XB 360 is a custom homebrew store for modded Xbox 360 consoles. It runs as a native application on your console, reads a catalog of homebrew from GitHub, and installs apps, games, emulators, and themes directly to your HDD.

Why it exists

The Xbox 360 homebrew scene has plenty of great content, but no single modern place to browse and install it. Anaconda XB 360 is an attempt to fix that: one menu, one catalog, one place to update from.

It also pulls from the Free60 Store (X-Store) alongside its own catalog, so you get both worlds without having to switch between two apps.

What makes it different

  • Native C++ .xex — not a Lua script. Full HTTP, real file I/O, no sandbox limits.
  • Catalog-driven — content lives in .ini files on GitHub. Adding a new app or theme doesn't need a new build.
  • External repos — you can point at any compatible catalog, not just the official one.
  • Portable — the entire app runs on a PC during development, so testing doesn't need a console.

Who builds it

One person. No team, no funding, no outside help. Progress is slow because of that, and updates come when they come. Please be patient.

The best way to help right now is to star the repo. That's the signal that people are interested, and it's the easiest way to be notified when a build goes public.

Status

Not public yet. Anaconda XB 360 is in early development. A first public build is planned but not scheduled. Star the repo to be notified.
